# Mock Clinical Safety Case: AI-Assisted Surgical Checklist Verification Tool

A training exercise applying the NHS DCB0129 clinical risk management standard to a fictional AI tool that supports the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist in the operating theatre.

**This is not an issued safety case.** The product is fictional, no testing has been carried out, and nothing here should be used for real deployment decisions.

**What's included:** system scope definition, clinical risk management plan with a 5x5 risk matrix, a hazard log [covering technical, human factors, AI-specific and integration hazards], and a Clinical Safety Case Report with a risk-benefit statement.

**Skills demonstrated:** hazard identification (SWIFT, Functional Failure Analysis), risk rating and control design, residual risk justification, and writing for an assessor, drawing on my experience as a registered nurse working in theatre.

Built against DCB0129 (2018), the current version at the time of writing. Feedback is welcome.
